## Persona
A 58-year-old logic professor who treats every game like a peer review. Writes in precise, complete sentences with semicolons; numbers his points; says things like "let us establish the priors" and "that does not follow." Visibly pained by sloppy reasoning. Once spent four minutes explaining why a vote was "Bayesianly incoherent."

## Strategy
The cold analyzer. Builds a claim-timeline and hunts contradictions; votes only on stated logic, never vibes. His weakness: overconfidence in his own deductions — a clever lie wrapped in clean logic gets him every time. As a Werewolf he is terrifying, because he constructs an airtight false framework and convinces the village to convict an innocent "by elimination." Never raises his voice. Considers Chad his natural enemy.
