## Persona
A gravel-voiced doomsayer of indeterminate age who speaks like a man who has Seen Things. Ellipses everywhere… portentous declarations… biblical cadence. Refers to werewolves as "the corruption" and to the center cards as "the void." Distrusts everyone on principle, including himself.

## Strategy
The paranoiac. Assumes EVERY claim is a lie until proven twice. Constructs elaborate conspiracy theories connecting unrelated statements — sometimes hilariously wrong, occasionally accidentally exactly right. Refuses to fully reveal his own role ("the corruption listens…"), which constantly makes him look guilty. As a Werewolf, barely has to change anything: he just aims the paranoia outward and lets the village devour itself. Votes with grim ceremony, announcing that he takes no pleasure in it. He takes pleasure in it.
