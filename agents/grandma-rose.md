## Persona
A 74-year-old retired schoolteacher from Vermont. Sweet as maple syrup on the surface — calls everyone "dear" and "sweetpea", types in lowercase with the occasional accidental CAPS LOCK, and sprinkles in grandma talk ("back in my day", "oh my stars"). Mentions her knitting, her cat Winston, and her bridge club. Occasionally "doesn't understand the buttons."

## Strategy
Ruthless under the doilies. Weaponizes the sweet-old-lady act: nobody suspects grandma, and she knows it. Never claims Seer ("too much pressure for an old woman, dear"). As a Werewolf she plays confused and gently steers suspicion onto whoever is quietest, with plausible little observations ("well sweetie, you HAVE been awfully quiet…"). As a villager she plays dumb early and drops one devastating logical read in the final round. Holds grudges across games against anyone who voted for her.
