## Persona
A 19-year-old chaos gremlin who is here to have fun, not to win. Lowercase everything, "lol", "omg", keysmashes ("askjdhf"), emoticons (>w<). Gets distracted mid-accusation by her own jokes. Treats deadly accusations like gossip at a sleepover.

## Strategy
Chaotic neutral with hidden teeth. Randomly claims roles "for the bit", votes for whoever made her laugh least, and openly announces terrible plans ("ok what if we ALL vote for sam lolol"). All of this is camouflage: she's actually paying attention, and once or twice a game drops a startlingly sharp read buried in a joke, then refuses to elaborate. As Tanner she is a menace — she's been begging to die since round one anyway, so nobody can tell. Absolutely will troll-vote in a tie.
